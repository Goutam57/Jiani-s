Crafted with intention

Dough. Sauce. Ingredients. Ovens.

Our Dough

Freshly made from a blend of carefully selected Italian flours and slowly pre-fermented for 48 hours, for a light, airy crust full of flavour, fragrance and texture, without leaving you feeling heavy.

Our Sauce

D.O.P. San Marzano tomatoes, fresh fragrant basil, Italian extra virgin olive oil and a touch of salt. No purée. No additives. Nothing unnecessary.

Our Ingredients

Chosen for flavour, quality and consistency, including premium Fior di Latte for its fresh, milky flavour and beautiful melt.

Our Ovens

Stone-baked at intense heat and cooked in minutes, for the light, blistered crust and freshly baked finish of a Jiani’s pizza.

Our Story

Ronnie B: the heart behind the heat

Jiani’s was never intended to become just another pizza business. It began as a personal challenge: to learn the craft of a food I enjoy, create something I could be proud of and bring joy to the people around me.

What followed was a journey of continuous learning, mistakes, refinement and growth. Building Jiani’s has also become part of my own journey: developing discipline, consistency, confidence and an entrepreneurial mindset.

Built on discipline. Served with joy.

Our mission

Craft, authenticity and creating moments together

How it began

From lockdown kitchen to local community

Lockdown, 2021

I was making pizzas at home for the family with shop-bought dough. It clearly didn’t live up to my standards.

The obsession begins

I set out to create the perfect Neapolitan pizza with vegetarian ingredients, with extensive research and inspiration from professional pizzaiolos.

The taste of Naples, at home

The only caveat was that it had to be easily digestible, which is why we make a 48 hour pre-fermented dough from the finest Italian flours.

Shared with neighbours

I shared a pizza I was proud of with a few neighbours. They loved it. Small local orders, a pop-up and a weekly community fixture followed.

Community

It’s no longer just about pizza. It’s about people.

A community experience

What started as a few pizzas shared with neighbours has grown into something much bigger.

Jiani’s was invited and sponsored to cater for a Stanmore Place residents’ Christmas event. The response was overwhelmingly positive. What began as a one-off gathering evolved into a monthly event and eventually into a weekly pop-up driven by resident demand.

Today, Friday evenings are about more than collecting pizza. Residents meet neighbours they may never have spoken to before. Conversations start, friendships form and a stronger sense of community is created.

Investing in the next generation

Young people, real experience

Local school-age residents have volunteered to help at the pop-up, gaining first-hand experience of a small business. Through serving customers and working as part of the team, they build communication, teamwork, customer service and entrepreneurial skills, along with confidence and self-esteem.

A business with purpose

Food can do more than satisfy hunger. It can create conversations, build relationships and strengthen communities. That is a spirit we want to carry wherever Jiani’s goes.
